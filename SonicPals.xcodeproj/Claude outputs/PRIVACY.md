# Sonic Pals — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: September 6, 2026*

Sonic Pals is an augmented reality game. This page explains, plainly, what
information the app does — and does not — collect. Short version: it
doesn't collect any.

## What we collect

Sonic Pals does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. There is
no account creation, no sign-in, and no analytics, advertising, or tracking
of any kind built into the app.

## Camera & LiDAR

Sonic Pals uses your device's camera and, on supported devices, its LiDAR
scanner, to detect nearby surfaces and place the game's augmented reality
content in your real environment.

This camera and sensor data is processed entirely on your device, in real
time, only to power the game. It is never recorded, saved, uploaded, or
shared — not with us, and not with anyone else.

## No internet connection required

Sonic Pals does not send information over the internet. Every part of the
game runs locally on your device.

## Third-party services

Sonic Pals does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or data
services.

## Children's privacy

Sonic Pals does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including
children — because it does not collect information from anyone at all.

## Changes to this policy

If a future update to Sonic Pals changes what data the app accesses or how
it's handled, this page will be updated and the date at the top will
change to match.
