# Sonic Pals: Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 7, 2026

Sonic Pals does not collect any personal data. No account, no sign-in, no
analytics, no ads, no tracking.

## Camera and LiDAR

The app uses your camera and, on supported devices, LiDAR, to detect
surfaces and place AR content in your room. This data stays on your
device and is used only to run the game. It is never recorded, saved, or
sent anywhere.

## No internet needed

Sonic Pals doesn't send anything over the internet. Everything runs
locally on your device.

## Third parties

None. No analytics or ad services are used.

## Children

Sonic Pals doesn't collect data from anyone, including children, because
it doesn't collect data at all.

## Changes

If a future update changes what the app collects or how it works, this
page will be updated and the date above will change.
